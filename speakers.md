## Keynotes

JWOC 2026 will feature two keynote speakers.

### Alix Jeannerot {#alix-jeannerot}
*Nokia Bell Labs, France*

**Title:** AI-RAN: From the Physical Layer to the Control Layer

**Abstract:** In this talk, we will review recent work on the implementation of AI models at different layers of the radio access network. Starting with DeepReceivers at the PHY layer (L1), we will move up the stack to DeepSchedulers (L2) and, finally, discuss potential use cases of AI in the control layer (L3).

**Biography:** Alix Jeannerot received Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Communication Systems from École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), Lausanne, Switzerland, and obtained his Ph.D. from INSA Lyon, France, in 2024. He is currently a researcher in AI for radio systems in the AI for Wireless team at Nokia Bell Labs, France. His work mainly focuses on applications of AI methods to the medium access control (MAC) and radio resource control (RRC) layers of cellular networks.

### Lina Mroueh {#lina-mroueh}
*ISEP, France*

**Title:** Beyond Connectivity: AI, Edge, and the Future of IoT Networks

**Biography:** Lina Mroueh is currently a professor in the Signal, Image, and Telecommunications Department at ISEP. She obtained her engineering degree and Ph.D. in Digital Communications from Telecom Paris (formerly ENST Paris) in 2006 and 2010, respectively. Her Ph.D. was carried out in collaboration with Motorola Labs, Telecom Paris, and ETH Zurich. In 2010, she worked as a postdoctoral researcher in the Communication Theory Group at ETH Zurich and in the Comelec Department at Telecom Paris. Her research focuses on modeling frequency-selective fading channels, space-time code design, and multi-user systems.
